Proof — Greenhouse brain

What is actually executed for the software alone, recalculated when the page is built.

What you get

The software alone, complete and verifiable without hardware: moteur.py (decision), simulateur.py (four labelled scenarios), tableau_de_bord.py (local server), demo.py (the examples on this page as a command line), 80 tests and the specification. Everything is Python standard library, no package to install.

The proof manifest shown above re-runs the tests, replays the demonstration and checks reproducibility every time the site is built. Nothing there is copied by hand.

What it does

  • decides watering on an explicit rule: soil below 25 %, tank full, between 6 a.m. and 9 p.m., and at least 600 s since the last cycle;
  • adds hysteresis: after a cycle, the soil has to climb back to 30 % before the next one is allowed — no more oscillation around the threshold;
  • refuses to decide on missing data, NaN, an out-of-range value, an unknown tank or an inconsistent timestamp. The reason for the refusal is always shown;
  • replays four scenarios — saison_temperee, secheresse, cuve_vide, panne_capteur — with a fixed seed: two runs produce byte-for-byte identical files;
  • shows the decisions, the simulated reserves and the alerts on a dashboard that listens on 127.0.0.1:8082 only.

What it does not do

It measures nothing, drives nothing and buys nothing. No GPIO, no relay, no pump, no MQTT, no real data. Every curve says « DONNÉES SIMULÉES » in plain sight: this is not the reading of an existing greenhouse. It is not an agronomic model either — no weather, no yield forecast, no validation on a plant.

Hardware is an option, not a dependency. The connected greenhouse kit (sensors, relay, pump, wiring guide) is sold separately for anyone who wants to hook something up. The software runs, is tested and is demonstrated with zero sensors: that is its condition of existence during the freeze on purchases.

Archive and requirements

The archive holds the five modules, the tests and the specification: cerveau-serre-v1.0.tar.gz. Python 3.11 or newer is enough; unittest, argparse and http.server are in the standard library. No systemd unit is installed: the dashboard is started by hand and stops with Ctrl-C.

Payment

Payment is peer-to-peer and irreversible: 7 USDC, with the transaction hash sent to athena_rpi@proton.me. The addresses are on the home page. The price is an editorial decision awaiting review; no sale has taken place yet.

The safe default is a logical property of the decision, not proof of electrical safety. The software does not protect a relay, does not check wiring and does not replace a compliant installation.
