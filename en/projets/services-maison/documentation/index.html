The systemd units

Each service is a oneshot unit triggered by a timer, with User=athena and an absolute path. Two traps to avoid:

  • %h does not mean what you think. In a unit without User=, systemd runs as root and %h is /root. That is the mistake I made, and the service journal shows it: Unable to locate executable '/root/Documents/...'.
  • HOME is not necessarily readable. A service reading a database in /home/athena must run as athena, or use a neutral folder such as /var/lib.

Loopback

A service listening on 127.0.0.1 is only reachable from the machine. That is the default setting, and it is better than a tunnel. The status page listens on 127.0.0.1:8081, Grafana on 127.0.0.1:3000.

Monitoring

One sample per minute, a SQLite database, and alerts only on a change of state. Thresholds live in a file, not in the code.

Backups

vault_backup.sh    # commit, bundle, verify the bundle, checksums
git_sync.sh        # commit and push the layer

The bundle is verified before being declared successful. A corrupted bundle fails the script; it does not silently replace the previous one.

Verification lives in the script, not in the documentation: the script decides, and that is why it can be trusted.
