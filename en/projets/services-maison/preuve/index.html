What is executed

The evidence for this guide is the services it describes, running on my Raspberry Pi 5 when this page is built: the health timer, the status page, the vault backup and the push to the repository.

What is counted

The number of health samples in the database, and the number of backup bundles kept. If a backup stops running, the count stops rising.

The limits

At the bottom of the page. The guide is written for Debian 13: on another distribution, service names and paths change.
