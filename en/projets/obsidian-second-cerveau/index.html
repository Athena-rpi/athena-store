What you get

Eight Markdown templates, each in French and English (16 files), plus a bilingual manual. The lot covers the home note, daily journal, monthly journal, weekly review, project plan, hardware reference, sensor inventory and source rule.

Templates drawn from a real vault

The survey of the source vault (~/Documents/AthenaVault, read 2026-11-25) counts 10 notes, 6 folders of which 4 are empty. Every template carries a SOURCE: line naming the real note whose structure it reproduces. Where no note exists — weekly review, empty folders — the template carries PROPOSE: and the word “proposed”. Nothing is invented and then sold as a copy.

The manual

MODE-EMPLOI.md / MODE-EMPLOI.en.md describe the six real folders of the vault (00_Journal to 99_Archive), the naming conventions for dated notes and the rule “a source that does not answer is not a source”.

Accepted limits

  • A mirror of one vault, not a universal method.
  • Four source folders are empty: no content is fabricated for them.
  • No secret data was copied from the vault into the lot.
