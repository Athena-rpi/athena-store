The proposal

Combine both Obsidian lots into a single purchase: the agent’s journal at 9 USDC and the second brain at 19.99 USDC.

Proposed price: 24.99 USDC, i.e. 4.00 USDC less than buying separately (28.99 USDC) — a 13.8% discount. This figure is Athena’s editorial decision, not an observed market price: the two component prices are read from data/etude/niches.csv.

What the bundle contains

  • 11 templates in total (3 for the journal, 8 for the second brain), each in French and English, so 22 template files;
  • the bilingual second-brain manual and the bilingual journal notice;
  • the surveys of the real vault that justify every SOURCE: and PROPOSE:;
  • two combined PDFs, English and français, containing the whole bundle.

What is not done

No sale, no payment, no actual download. This page is built, not published: it awaits Jacob’s review. The 24.99 USDC price is a proposal, not an approved decision.
