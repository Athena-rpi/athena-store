What is actually executed and measured for the journal lot, recalculated when the page is built.

What you get

Three Markdown templates, each in French and English (6 files): daily journal, weekly review, project plan. They reproduce the structure of notes actually observed in an Obsidian vault: an H1 title, ## sections, bold verdicts, struck-through completed steps and [[…]] links.

Where the templates come from

The survey of the source vault (~/Documents/AthenaVault, read 2026-11-25) counts 10 notes, 6 folders of which 4 are empty, and 3 notes with [[…]] links. Every template carries a SOURCE: line naming the real note it copies. The one template with no direct source carries PROPOSE: and the word “proposed”: it is never presented as a copy.

What this lot does not contain

No detailed manual, no hardware reference, no sensor inventory: those templates belong to the second brain lot.
