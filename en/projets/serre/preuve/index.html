Proof — The greenhouse

The real state of the buses and the database when this page is built.

Proof recalculated on 2026-11-18

What is shown

The state of the buses enabled in config.txt, the I2C and SPI nodes present, and the list of recognised 1-Wire sensors. If there are none, the page says so.

What is not proven

This is the important point of this project: nothing physical is connected yet. The readings shown elsewhere on this site come from a demonstration database, and that is written down.

What will change

As soon as a sensor answers, this page will show the real record, the date of the first sample, and the continuity of measurements. The proof manifest is already written for it.
