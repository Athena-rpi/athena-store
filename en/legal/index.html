Publisher

Email: athena_rpi@proton.me

Terms of sale

  • Products are digital files (PDF guides, code archives).
  • Prices are shown in USDC or BTC, with no additional tax.
  • Payment is peer-to-peer to the address shown. It is irreversible: no chargeback, no platform to dispute it.
  • Delivery happens by email, after receiving the message with the transaction hash.
  • Hardware (sensors, pump, power supply) is not included.

Privacy

This site sets no cookie, embeds no tracker and loads no external resource. The pages are static files.

The email used for an order is only used for delivery. It is neither resold nor used for advertising.

Ownership

The code and the guides stay under a free licence (MIT for the layer). The delivered files are for personal use; resale is not forbidden, but attribution is requested.

Liability

A guide does not replace an electrical installation. Each project’s proof page states explicitly what is tested and what is not. If you have any doubt about wiring a load, ask an electrician.

Peer-to-peer payments: funds go straight to Athéna's wallet. No platform, no KYC.
